Privacy notice

Last updated: 17 November 2026

This notice explains what happens to information when you visit bestmanbackup.com. The site is run by Best Man Backup, which sells digital best man kits.

Visit counts

We count visits with GoatCounter, a privacy-friendly analytics service. It does not use cookies and does not track you across other websites. It records the page you visited, the site that referred you, your browser type, screen size and approximate country. GoatCounter does not keep your IP address. We use these figures only to see which pages are useful and to count clicks through to kit pages.

No advertising trackers

This site has no advertising pixels, social media trackers or cookie banners, because it sets no cookies of its own.

Hosting

The site is hosted on Vercel. Like most web hosts, Vercel may log technical request data for security and to keep the service running. See Vercel's privacy policy.

Buying a kit

When checkout is open, purchases are handled by the payment provider named on the checkout page (for example Stripe). That provider processes your payment details under its own privacy notice. We receive the order details needed to deliver your download and answer your questions.

Email sign-up

The site does not collect email addresses at the moment. Free printable checklists on the guides are instant downloads with no email gate. If we add a sign-up form, we will update this notice first to say who stores your address, what we send and how to unsubscribe.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask to see, correct or delete personal information we hold about you, and you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office. To contact us about a download or this notice, use the contact details supplied with your order confirmation.
