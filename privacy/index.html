Privacy notice

Last updated: 17 October 2026

This notice explains what happens to information when you visit bestmanbackup.com. The site is run by Best Man Backup, which sells digital best man kits through its Etsy shop.

Visit counts

We count visits with GoatCounter, a privacy-friendly analytics service. It does not use cookies and does not track you across other websites. It records the page you visited, the site that referred you, your browser type, screen size and approximate country. GoatCounter does not keep your IP address. We use these figures only to see which pages are useful and to count clicks through to our Etsy listings.

No advertising trackers

This site has no advertising pixels, social media trackers or cookie banners, because it sets no cookies of its own.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like most web hosts, GitHub may log visitors' IP addresses for security and to keep the service running. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Buying a kit

All purchases happen on Etsy. When you buy, Etsy handles your payment and account details under Etsy's privacy policy. As the seller we receive the order details Etsy shares with sellers, such as your name and the items you bought, and use them only to deliver your order and answer your questions.

Email sign-up

The site does not collect email addresses at the moment. If we add a sign-up form, we will update this notice first to say who stores your address, what we send and how to unsubscribe.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask to see, correct or delete personal information we hold about you, and you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office. To contact us, message the BestManBackup shop on Etsy.
