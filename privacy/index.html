Privacy notice

Last updated: 28 November 2026

This notice explains what happens to information when you visit bestmanbackup.com. The site is run by Best Man Backup, which sells digital best man kits.

Visit counts

We count visits with GoatCounter, a privacy-friendly analytics service. It does not use cookies and does not track you across other websites. It records the page you visited, the site that referred you, your browser type, screen size and approximate country. GoatCounter does not keep your IP address. We use these figures only to see which pages are useful and to count clicks through to kit pages.

No advertising trackers

This site has no advertising pixels, social media trackers or cookie banners, because it sets no cookies of its own.

Hosting

The site is hosted on Vercel. Like most web hosts, Vercel may log technical request data for security and to keep the service running. See Vercel's privacy policy.

Buying a kit

Purchases are handled by Stripe. Stripe processes your payment details under its own privacy notice. We receive the order details needed to deliver your download and answer your questions.

Email sign-up

The free guide checklists and the pocket checklist are instant downloads. They do not ask for an email address.

The free cards do. If you ask for one, we store the email address you type, which card and paper size you chose, the time, and the fact that you ticked the consent box. We store that record in a private Vercel Blob store on the same host as the site. We use it to give you the card and, because you asked, to send the occasional note about the kits. We do not sell your address and we do not pass it to an advertising network.

The card downloads in your browser after the form is accepted. You can ask us to delete the address, or to stop the notes, by emailing hello@bestmanbackup.com.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask to see, correct or delete personal information we hold about you, and you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office. Email hello@bestmanbackup.com.
