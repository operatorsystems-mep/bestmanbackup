BEFORE YOU BUY

A few good questions

Which kit should I choose?

Choose Duties for your role, wedding-day responsibilities and the stag do budget; Playbook for games on the night; or Speech for writing and delivery. The Best Man Bundle covers the Duties Kit, the Stag Do & Bachelor Party Playbook and the Speech Kit together.

What software will I need?

The editable documents are Word files for a current desktop version of Microsoft Word. Read and print the PDF versions with a PDF reader. Word licences are not included.

Is this a printed book or a speech-writing service?

No. The kits are digital downloads you personalise yourself — not a printed book and not a speech-writing service.

Is anything posted to me?

These are digital downloads. Print the pages you need at home or through your preferred print service.

How do I get help with a download?

Download in a browser, extract the ZIP and open Start Here. If you need help, email hello@bestmanbackup.com with your order time and the filename you are opening.
