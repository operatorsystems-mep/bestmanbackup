Questions

What software do I need?

A PDF reader for the PDFs, and Microsoft Word or a compatible editor for the Word files. The money tracker opens in Excel, Numbers or Google Sheets.

Can I use it on my phone?

You can read the PDFs on a phone. Editing and printing work best on a laptop.

How do I get the files?

Straight after payment you get a private download link for a ZIP. Problems? hello@bestmanbackup.com

Will you write my speech for me?

No. The Speech Kit is a toolkit you use yourself: you bring the stories and it gives you the structure, prompts and cue cards.
