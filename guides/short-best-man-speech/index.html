SHORT BEST MAN SPEECH

Short best man speech: how to write a 2–3 minute speech for a UK wedding

THE SHORT ANSWER

Two to three minutes is the right length for a short best man speech at a UK wedding, roughly 260 to 390 written words at around 130 words a minute. Two minutes is a sensible minimum. That matches the length Toastmasters recommends for a toast. A standard UK speech still runs five to seven minutes if you have more room.

  • Short speech or toast: 2–3 minutes, about 260–390 words
  • US reception toast: 3–5 minutes
  • Standard UK speech: 5–7 minutes (see how long a best man speech should be)
  • Joint best men: 7–10 minutes combined

Keep the same shape as a full speech but compress it: a one-line opening, who you are, one well-chosen story, a line about the couple, then the toast. Short works when every line earns its place.

When a short speech is the right call

A short speech isn't a lazy speech. It's often exactly what the day needs:

  • The couple asked for it. Some couples want brief speeches so the party starts sooner.
  • There are lots of speeches. If you're the fourth or fifth speaker, two or three minutes will feel like a gift.
  • It's a small or informal wedding. A long, formal speech in a pub garden can feel out of place.
  • You're sharing the job. In a joint speech, each best man's part is effectively a short speech.
  • You're anxious about speaking. A tight, well-rehearsed two or three minutes is better than a nervous ten.
  • It's a US-style toast. Reception toasts tend to be shorter.

The structure of a short speech

PartWhat to sayTime
Opening (opening lines guide)One or two lines that get a smile20–30 seconds
Who you areName and how you know the groom20–30 seconds
One storyA specific story that shows who he isAbout 1–1½ minutes
The coupleWhat changed when he met his partner, plus something about them30–45 seconds
ToastOne sincere line, then both names15–30 seconds

That's it. No long thank-yous and no warm-up. One story, told properly. If you have two that make the same point, keep the stronger one.

A worked example

Good evening, everyone. I'm Sam, Tom's best man. He asked me to keep this short, so I've cut out all the good bits.

I've known Tom since we were seven, when he traded me his entire sticker collection for a single conker. It was not a good conker. But he said it "looked like it had potential". That's Tom. He sees the best in everything, and everyone, even when the evidence suggests otherwise.

Then he met Jess, and for once the potential was obvious to all of us. Jess, you're kind, you're funny and you're the only person who's ever got him to plan a holiday more than a week in advance. We're so glad you're part of the family.

So please raise your glasses. To Tom and Jess.

That's about 160 words, a little over a minute spoken with laughs. Add a second detail to the story and a few lines about the friendship and you're at a comfortable two to three minutes, roughly 260 to 390 words.

How to write it

  1. Write your full speech first, then cut. It's easier to cut a five-minute speech down than to build a short one from nothing. Draft in the speech template, then keep only the strongest bits.
  2. Pick one story that shows character. It should say something about who he is, not just something funny that happened. Our how to write a best man speech guide has prompts for finding it.
  3. Make the story specific. One precise detail ("a single conker") does more work than a paragraph of general description.
  4. Link the story to the couple. If your story shows he's loyal, the couple section can say he's found someone who deserves that loyalty.
  5. End sincerely. A short speech has no room for a wobble at the end. Write the toast word for word.

Common mistakes in short speeches

  • Rushing the delivery. Short doesn't mean fast. Speak slowly and pause after laughs; it's still a speech.
  • Cutting the partner. If anything gets cut, it shouldn't be the line about the partner.
  • A list of quick jokes. Five one-liners in a row feel like a stand-up audition. One story is warmer.
  • Ending on "anyway, that's me". Always end on the toast.
  • Underpreparing. A short speech still needs rehearsing. Every flubbed word stands out more.

Short speeches for special cases

Joint best men

If you're sharing, each of you can take a short section: one does the opening and a story, the other does a second story, the couple and the toast. See our joint best man speech guide.

Brothers

A short brother's speech can lean on one childhood story and one line about what it means to welcome someone into the family. Our brother speech guide has ideas.

When you've been asked last-minute

If you've been asked at short notice, don't panic. Write the five parts in the table above on one card, rehearse three times out loud, and go. Our nerves guide helps with the day itself.

How short is too short?

A minute or less risks feeling rushed or impersonal, as Zola points out, unless it's clearly just a toast. Two minutes is a sensible minimum. The standard UK length is five to seven minutes. For the full picture on length, read how long a best man speech should be.

Frequently asked questions

How long is a short best man speech?

Around two to three minutes, roughly 260 to 390 written words at about 130 words a minute. For the full length ladder (short, standard UK, US toast, joint), see how long a best man speech should be.

Is a short best man speech rude?

No. Many couples prefer short speeches, and a well-written two- or three-minute speech is often more memorable than a long one.

What should a short best man speech include?

An opening line, who you are, one specific story about the groom, a line about the couple and the partner, and a toast.

Can a best man just give a toast instead of a speech?

Yes, if the couple are happy with it. A brief toast with a sincere line and both names is perfectly acceptable, especially at small or informal weddings. See our best man toast guide for ready-made lines.

How do I cut a best man speech down to two or three minutes?

Write the full draft first, then keep one story, drop long thank-yous and warm-up jokes, and end on the toast. Use the five-part structure above and the speech template so every remaining line earns its place.
