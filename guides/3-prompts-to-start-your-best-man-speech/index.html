BEST MAN SPEECH PROMPTS

3 prompts to start your best man speech

THE SHORT ANSWER

Start with three short writing prompts, not a blank page. Answer (1) how you met the groom, (2) one story that shows who he is with his partner, and (3) the toast you want to land in twenty words or fewer. Those three answers give you an opening, a middle and an ending. Then shape them into a full speech with a template or our step-by-step how-to-write guide.

Why prompts beat a blank page

Most best men stall because they try to invent jokes first. The room does not need a comedian. It needs someone who knows the groom and can say so clearly. Three timed answers on paper beat an evening of staring at a laptop.

Set a timer for ten minutes. Write in full sentences. Do not edit while you draft. You can cut later when you check how long a best man speech should be or trim for a short best man speech.

Prompt 1: the friendship origin

Write for three minutes:

"I met [groom's name] when… What has not changed about him since then is… What has changed is…"

Fill every blank with something specific: a place, a year, a habit, a nickname. Skip anything that needs a ten-minute explanation for guests who were not there.

This block often becomes your first thirty seconds: who you are, how you know him, and a light first smile. For ready-made opener shapes once you have the material, see our best man speech opening lines guide.

Worked sketch (swap the details):

"I met Tom on our first day of secondary school, when he asked to borrow a pen. What has not changed is that he still talks his way into every room. What has changed is that he finally met someone who can talk him back out of trouble."

Prompt 2: one story that shows who he is

Write for four minutes:

"Pick one moment that shows who [groom] is when he is with [partner]. Start with 'I remember when…' Keep it under 120 words. End with one line about what that moment says about them as a couple."

Choose a story that works for grandparents and mates alike. If you have to warn half the room first, pick another story. One clear story beats three half-told ones.

Useful filters before you keep it:

  • Would the partner laugh with him, or only at him?
  • Can a guest who has never met either of them follow it in one telling?
  • Does it end kindly?

If the story fails those checks, drop it. Our notes on what not to say in a best man speech cover the usual traps.

Prompt 3: write the toast first

Write for three minutes:

"Write the last sentence of your speech first: the toast. Keep it under twenty words. Name the couple. Wish them something specific, not 'a lifetime of happiness' on its own. Then list three words that your stories need to earn that toast."

Working backwards from the toast stops speeches that wander and then bolt a generic ending on. Once you have the toast, every story either earns those three words or it gets cut.

Example toast seed:

"To Tom and Priya: may you keep making each other braver. Cheers."

Three words that toast might need from the stories: brave, kind, steady. If a story does not support those words, it does not belong in this speech.

How to turn the answers into a speech

You now have raw material. Shape it in this order:

  1. Opening. Use Prompt 1 plus a short self-introduction. Practise the first two lines from memory.
  2. Middle. Use Prompt 2 as your main story. Add a second only if you still have time after a rehearsal aloud.
  3. Turn and toast. Use Prompt 3. Look at the couple, raise the glass, stop talking.

A standard UK speech sits at five to seven minutes. If the couple have asked for something shorter, aim for two to three. Put the cut version on cue cards and rehearse standing up.

Frequently asked questions

How do I start writing a best man speech?

Do not begin with jokes or the dictionary definition of marriage. Answer three short prompts on paper: how you met the groom, one story that shows who he is, and the toast you want to land. Those three answers become your opening, your middle and your ending.

What are good prompts for a best man speech?

Use prompts that pull out real details: when you met him, one moment that shows his character with his partner, and a toast of twenty words or fewer. Vague prompts like "write something funny" stall most writers.

How long should a best man speech be once I have started?

At a UK wedding, aim for five to seven minutes (about 650 to 900 words). A short speech can run two to three minutes. Write first, then cut to time. See our length guide and short speech guide.

Do these three prompts replace a full speech structure?

No. They get you unstuck and give you raw material. You still need a clear opening, one or two stories, a warm turn toward the couple and a toast. Use a template or the Speech Kit for the full structure and cue cards.
