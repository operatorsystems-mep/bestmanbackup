DUTIES AND THE WEDDING DAY

Best man checklist: the week before the wedding

Updated 17 November 2026

About these guides: written by Best Man Backup, an independent site for best men only.

THE SHORT ANSWER

Use the final week to confirm, not invent. Lock the speech onto cue cards, confirm the rings plan, recheck outfits and travel, confirm the rehearsal, brief the ushers, and put timings and contacts on one sheet you will carry. The day before: charge your phone, check the route, sleep.

Why the week before is different

Month-by-month planning belongs in our best man timeline. The full role list is in the duties checklist. This page is only the final seven days: the jobs that go wrong when they are left to the morning.

Seven days out

  • Confirm ceremony and reception timings with the couple or planner.
  • Confirm who holds the rings and when you collect them. See best man rings.
  • Confirm speech order and your slot. See wedding speech order.
  • Message the ushers or groomsmen with call times and dress code.
  • Do a full standing read of the speech with a timer. Cut anything that still trips you.

Three to four days out

  • Move the speech onto numbered cue cards. Large type. Toast wording exact. See cue cards.
  • Try on the full outfit: jacket, shoes, cufflinks, tie. Fix gaps now.
  • Print or save a one-page day sheet: timings, venue addresses, taxi numbers, vendor contacts.
  • Confirm transport for the groom’s side to the ceremony.
  • If gifts or cards are your job, buy and wrap them.

The day before

  • Charge your phone and a small power bank.
  • Check roadworks, rail strikes and parking for the ceremony route.
  • Attend the rehearsal if there is one. Practise the ring hand-over.
  • Eat a normal meal. Go easy on the drink.
  • Lay out your clothes and put cards, pouch and day sheet in the jacket.
  • Text the groom something calm and useful, not a joke that needs a reply at midnight.

US notes

If there is a rehearsal dinner, confirm whether you speak there, at the reception, or both. A rehearsal dinner toast is shorter than the wedding speech; see our rehearsal dinner toast guide. Bachelor party money should already be settled before this week.

Frequently asked questions

What should a best man do the week before the wedding?

Confirm timings, rings, speech slot and outfits; move the speech to cue cards; brief ushers; prepare a one-page day sheet; then rest the night before.

When should I finish my best man speech?

Have a full draft earlier, but lock the final cut and cue cards three to four days out so you are rehearsing the version you will give.

What should I check the day before?

Route and travel, phone charge, rehearsal notes, clothes laid out, rings plan confirmed, and a sensible night’s sleep.

Do I need a physical checklist?

Yes. Group chats hide details. One printed or offline note with timings and contacts is enough.

What if I was asked last minute?

Use this week-before list as your whole plan, keep the speech short, and read our last-minute speech guide.
