BEST MAN COSTS

What does the best man pay for?

THE SHORT ANSWER

The best man usually pays for his own travel, accommodation, share of the stag do and a wedding gift. His suit depends on where you are: in the UK the couple traditionally pay for the groomsmen's suits, though many groomsmen now pay their own; in the US groomsmen usually buy or rent their own.

The couple usually pay for the wedding itself, and by custom the stag group splits the groom's share of the stag do. Customs vary between families and countries, so have an early, honest conversation with the groom rather than assuming.

Why it's worth talking about early

Money is a common source of quiet tension in a wedding party. Nobody wants to be the one who asks, so people guess, and guesses differ. A short conversation with the groom early on, about what he expects and what the couple are covering, saves a lot of awkwardness later.

Good questions to ask:

  • Are you covering outfits for the wedding party, or are we sorting our own?
  • Is accommodation for the night of the wedding being covered or booked as a block?
  • What kind of stag do are you picturing: a night out, a weekend, abroad?
  • Is there a group gift from the groomsmen, and roughly how much should it be?

What the best man usually pays for

  • Getting there. Travel to the wedding and any accommodation, unless the couple have offered to cover it.
  • What he wears, sometimes. In the UK, Hitched says it's traditionally the couple who pay for the groomsmen's suits, but many groomsmen now pay for their own unless the couple want matching outfits. In the US, The Knot says groomsmen normally buy or rent their own suit or tux and accessories. Ask early.
  • His share of the stag do or bachelor party. Plus, by common custom, a share of the groom's costs, split between everyone going.
  • A wedding gift. Some best men also give a personal gift to the groom. Neither has to be expensive.
  • Small on-the-day costs. Taxi money, a spare phone charger, the odd round. Keep a little cash or a card handy.
  • Pre-wedding events. Travel to fittings, the rehearsal, or an engagement party if there is one.

Do best men pay for their suits?

It depends on the country and on the couple. In the UK, Hitched says the couple traditionally pay for the groomsmen's suits, but many groomsmen now pay for their own unless the couple want matching outfits. In the US, The Knot says groomsmen normally buy or rent their own suit or tux and accessories, while the couple usually cover any accessories they want the wedding party to match.

So ask the groom before you book or buy anything: is he covering suits, is there a matching look, and is it hire or buy? If you're paying, look for hire deals early, or ask whether a suit you already own is fine. If it's hire, agree who returns the suits after the wedding.

What the couple usually pay for

  • The ceremony, venue, food and drink at the wedding.
  • Buttonholes, wedding-day transport, and any accessories they want the wedding party to match (The Knot).
  • Outfits, traditionally, in the UK, especially if they've chosen a matching look. In the US the groomsmen usually pay.
  • A thank-you gift for the best man and other members of the wedding party.
  • Sometimes accommodation for the wedding party on the night, though this varies.

The stag do or bachelor party

This is usually the biggest cost, and the best man is often the one collecting money. For a sense of scale, stag operator StagWeb reported an average spend of £195 a head on UK stag dos in 2024 (£214 abroad). StagWeb's figure covers accommodation, activities and nightlife, not transport or food, so check what any quoted price includes. The Knot's 2023 survey put the average US bachelor party at about $1,500 per attendee. Yours doesn't need to be anywhere near that. A few ways to keep it fair:

  • Set a budget before booking anything. Ask the group privately what they can afford and plan to the lower end.
  • Be clear about the groom's share. Hitched says couples traditionally don't pay for their own stag or hen, but covering him should be a group decision, not an assumption. Say upfront roughly how much it adds.
  • Collect deposits early, with at least a payday's notice, as Hitched suggests. It stops one person carrying all the cost for weeks.
  • Offer a lower-cost option. Let people join for one day or one evening instead of a whole weekend.
  • Keep a simple record. Who's paid what, and what's still owed. A shared note or spreadsheet is enough, or the Best Man Duties Kit on Etsy (opens in a new tab) has planner sections for the stag do budget, supplier bookings and guest payments (you do the sums; nothing calculates automatically).
  • Avoid fronting large sums yourself. Where you can, ask people to pay suppliers directly or pay you before you book.

Destination weddings

If the wedding is abroad, costs rise quickly: flights, several nights of accommodation, perhaps time off work. The same principle applies: the wedding party usually pays its own travel and stay unless the couple say otherwise. If it's a stretch, say so early. Couples planning a destination wedding generally expect that some people will need to make choices, and would rather know.

UK and US differences

In both countries the wedding party usually covers its own travel and its share of the stag do or bachelor party, and the couple cover the wedding. The biggest difference is outfits: UK tradition has the couple paying, while US custom has groomsmen paying for their own. In practice it varies a lot, so always ask rather than assume.

Gifts: what's expected

For the couple

A wedding gift from the best man is normal, but it doesn't need to be extravagant. The Knot's 2023 guest study found US wedding-party members spent an average of $160 on gifts, and clubbing together for a group gift is perfectly acceptable (The Knot). If there's a gift list, use it. A thoughtful, personal gift or a contribution to a honeymoon fund is just as welcome.

For the groom

Some best men give the groom a small personal gift on the morning: a card, something with a shared memory, a good bottle. Entirely optional.

Group gifts

Groomsmen sometimes club together. Agree the amount early and let people opt in privately.

If money is tight

Say so early and privately. Most grooms would far rather know than find out later. Ways to keep your costs down:

  • Suggest a UK or local stag do instead of abroad, or a single day instead of a weekend.
  • Look for outfit hire deals early, or wear a suit you already own if the couple are happy.
  • Share accommodation with other guests.
  • Make, rather than buy, a personal gift: a framed photo, a letter.

Being a great best man is about turning up, keeping things running and saying something kind about him, not spending the most. That speech doesn't need to be long either: our guide to a short best man speech shows how to say it well in two to three minutes.

Frequently asked questions

Do best men pay for their own suits?

It depends. In the UK the couple traditionally pay for groomsmen's suits, though many groomsmen now pay for their own. In the US groomsmen usually buy or rent their own. Check before booking.

Who pays for the suits if the couple want matching outfits?

In the UK, Hitched says many groomsmen now pay for their own suits unless the couple want matching outfits, so agree a matching look with the couple before anyone books. In the US groomsmen usually still buy or rent their own, while the couple usually cover accessories they want everyone to match.

Does the best man pay for the stag do?

The best man pays his own share, and by custom the group usually splits the groom's share between them. The best man doesn't normally pay for everyone.

Does the groom pay on a stag do?

Traditionally not. The group usually splits his share, but Hitched advises making that a group decision, especially for an expensive trip abroad.

How much does a stag do cost per person?

StagWeb reported an average spend of £195 a head on UK stag dos in 2024 (£214 abroad). StagWeb's figure covers accommodation, activities and nightlife, not transport or food, so check what any quoted price includes. Yours doesn't need to be anywhere near that: set a budget with the group before booking.

Does the best man buy a wedding gift?

Usually yes, though it doesn't need to be expensive. Some groomsmen club together for a group gift instead.

How much should the best man spend on a wedding gift?

There's no set amount. The Knot's 2023 guest study found US wedding-party members spent an average of $160 on gifts, and a group gift or a thoughtful personal gift is perfectly acceptable.

Does the groom pay for the best man's accommodation?

Not usually, but some couples cover or block-book rooms for the wedding party. Ask rather than assume.

Who pays for the best man's travel to a destination wedding?

Usually the best man. The wedding party normally pays its own travel and stay unless the couple say otherwise. If it's a stretch, say so early.

Who pays for the buttonholes and the rings?

The couple. Buttonholes are usually part of the couple's costs, and the rings are theirs too; the best man only looks after them.

What if I can't afford to be best man?

Tell the groom early and privately. Most grooms would rather adjust plans, such as a cheaper stag do, than have you struggle in silence.
