WRITING THE SPEECH

Best man speech for a second marriage: what to say

No ex jokes, no 'second time lucky'. Here's what to say instead.

Updated 17 November 2026

THE SHORT ANSWER

Write it like any good best man speech, but keep every line about this couple and this day. Introduce yourself, tell one or two stories that show who he is now, say something specific about his partner, then toast them. Skip jokes about divorce, “trying again” or comparing weddings. If children or a blended family are part of their life, a warm sentence is enough.

What changes for a second marriage speech

The structure does not change. You still open, say who you are, tell a story, turn to the couple and raise a glass. What changes is the risk: the room already knows there was a before. Your job is to celebrate the marriage in front of you without turning the past into material.

That is true whether this is his second wedding, hers, or both. Guests from the first chapter may be in the room. Children may be in the room. Write as if every line will be heard by someone who lived that history.

  • Present tense wins. “He is steadier with her” beats “he finally got it right.”
  • One story from this relationship is worth more than a timeline of his life.
  • Name the partner early so the speech is about the couple, not a friendship retrospective.
  • Match the tone of the day. Second weddings are often smaller and more relaxed; a short, warm speech fits.

What never to say

  • Exes, by name or by implication.
  • Divorce jokes, court jokes, or “round two” punchlines.
  • “You finally found the right one” or “this time it’ll stick.”
  • Comparisons between partners, weddings or families.
  • Advice about how to make marriage work after a hard chapter.

If a line needs the first marriage to make sense, cut it. Our full list of what not to say in a best man speech still applies; the second-marriage filter just sits on top.

A simple structure

PartWhat to sayTime
OpeningWho you are, how you know him, one light line20–30 seconds
Who he is nowOne specific story that shows character in this chapter1–2 minutes
The coupleWhat you have seen since his partner arrived; include children if they are part of the day45–60 seconds
ToastBoth names, one forward-looking wish15–20 seconds

Aim for three to five minutes, or shorter if the couple asked for brief speeches. See how long a best man speech should be and the short best man speech guide if you need to cut hard.

Opening lines you can adapt

Keep the open light and present. Swap the names.

  • “Evening. I’m Sam, Tom’s best man. I’ve known him long enough to know when he is performing and when he is himself. Today he is himself.”
  • “I’m Priya. I’ve watched Tom make a lot of decisions. This is one I can stand up and cheer for without a note.”
  • “Hello. I’m Dan. Tom asked me to keep this about today, so that is exactly what you’re getting.”
  • “I’m Alex. For anyone who only knows Tom from work: the version of him beside Jess is the best one.”

Stories that work

Pick material from this relationship, or from a trait that still shows up with his partner in the frame.

  • A Tuesday story. How he shows up on an ordinary week: the lift, the hospital car park, the late train he waited for.
  • The first time you met his partner and what you noticed without being told.
  • A habit that softens when they are together — lateness, stubbornness, overpacking — without making the partner the punchline.
  • Blended family, if true. One sentence about how he is with the children, or how they have built a household, then move on. Do not narrate custody or logistics.

If you were also best man last time, you do not owe the room that fact. If you mention it at all, keep it to one dry line and pivot immediately to this couple.

A worked example

About 320 words. Swap the names and the kitchen story for yours.

Good evening. I’m Miles, and I’ve known Jack since a flatshare that taught both of us how to live with other people’s cereal. Jack asked me to keep this about today, which is a kindness to all of us.

The thing about Jack is that he prepares for other people and forgets himself. When Maya moved flats he arrived with labels, a screwdriver and a list of what she was likely to lose. He did not bring a list for his own boxes. Two weeks later he was still eating off a plate he had meant to unpack, and he had built her an entire cupboard. That is still him. He makes a room feel looked after.

Maya, you seem to have noticed without needing a speech to explain it. Since you arrived he has been earlier, kinder to the group chat, and oddly proud of a Sunday roast that is only sometimes on time. You make him look up from the list. We are glad — not in the polite way, in the way that means the friendship is safer because you are in it.

Please raise your glasses. To Jack and Maya: may the lists stay useful, and may you keep being the reason he puts them down.

Closing and the toast

End on the couple, not on history. Both names. One concrete wish. Then sit down. Ready-made closers are in how to end a best man speech and the toast guide.

Frequently asked questions

Should a best man mention a previous marriage?

Usually no. If the first marriage is obvious and a single respectful sentence helps the room settle, say it once and pivot to this couple. Do not joke about it.

How long should a best man speech for a second marriage be?

Three to five minutes is plenty. Many second weddings prefer shorter speeches, so check with the couple and see our short speech guide if you need two to three minutes.

What if there are children from a previous relationship?

A warm, specific sentence about how he is with them — or how the household works together — is welcome. Skip logistics, blame and anything that puts a child on the spot.

Can the speech still be funny?

Yes. Aim the humour at safe habits: cooking, lateness, a sports team. Never at marriage, divorce or “trying again.”

I was best man at the first wedding. Do I say so?

You do not have to. If you do, keep it to one light line and move straight to why this day matters.
