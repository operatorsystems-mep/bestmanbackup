THE STAG DO

Bachelor party checklist for the best man

Updated 17 November 2026

About these guides: written by Best Man Backup, an independent site for best men only.

THE SHORT ANSWER

Start with the groom’s limits and guest list, set a per-person budget, poll dates, collect deposits before you book, then lock lodging, travel, the headline activity and a simple itinerary. Keep one shared note of confirmations. UK readers: this is the same job as the stag do under a US name.

As soon as you say yes

  • Ask the groom: vibe, hard noes, surprise level, and who must be invited.
  • Decide who is organising — you alone, or you plus one co-pilot.
  • Create one group chat or email thread for attendees only.

Zola and The Knot both put the groom conversation first for a reason: fixing a destination before limits wastes money.

Three to six months out

  • Draft a guest list the groom has approved.
  • Agree a budget range per person before destinations harden.
  • Shortlist two or three date options; pick the one most people can do.
  • Choose local vs destination.
  • If travel is involved, sketch flights and rooms before activities.

Six to eight weeks out

  • Collect deposits from confirmed guests.
  • Book lodging for the confirmed headcount only.
  • Book the headline activity and any group dinner that needs a deposit.
  • Set one payment method and a balance deadline.
  • Plan a weather or closure backup.

Money mechanics mirror our stag do budget guide even when you are using Venmo or similar US tools.

One to two weeks out

  • Send a clear itinerary: addresses, times, what is prepaid, what is personal spend.
  • Reconfirm bookings.
  • Arrange airport meets or a rideshare plan.
  • Pack games, a small medical kit, portable chargers and a silly trophy if that is your group.
  • Assign a co-pilot for logistics so you can also be present.

During the party

  • Keep confirmation numbers offline.
  • Pace the day; do not stack every expensive thing into hour one.
  • The groom pays for nothing once the plan says the group covers him.
  • Stay sober enough to solve problems.

Frequently asked questions

Who plans the bachelor party?

Traditionally the best man, sometimes with other groomsmen helping. Agree it early so the work is not fuzzy.

When should a bachelor party happen?

Commonly one to three months before the wedding, not the night before. Confirm with the couple.

Does the groom pay?

Usually the group covers the groom’s share. Decide that when you set the per-person budget.

What is the difference between a stag do and a bachelor party?

Same job, different name: stag do is the usual UK term; bachelor party is the usual US term.

What should I collect before booking?

Deposits from confirmed guests. Do not book rooms for maybes.
