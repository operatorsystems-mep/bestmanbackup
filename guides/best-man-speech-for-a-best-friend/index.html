BEST MAN SPEECH FOR A FRIEND

Best man speech for a best friend: how to write it, with examples

Updated 4 November 2026

About these guides: written by Best Man Backup, an independent site for best men only.

THE SHORT ANSWER

A best man speech for a best friend works when you show why you chose each other, with one specific story and a real welcome for his partner. Childhood friends and friends who met as adults use different material. Neither needs a tour of every year you have known him.

Childhood friend, or a friend you met later

Best friend is not one kind of material. The speech changes with when the friendship started, because the room's question changes with it. A childhood friend is being asked, quietly, "what was he like before we knew him?" A friend from university, work or a five-a-side team is being asked, "why you?"

Answer the question you have actually been given.

If you grew up together

You have the long view, and you should use a little of it, not all of it. One childhood scene that is still true of him is worth more than a timeline from primary school to the proposal. The risk is the opposite of having nothing to say. You have too much, and a lot of it is only funny to the two of you. Pick the scene his partner would recognise. If he was loyal in a playground, and he is loyal now, that is the speech. If the best story needs a map of your old estate and four nicknames, it is not the speech.

You are not his brother. You did not share a house or a set of parents, unless you practically did. Do not borrow family authority you have not got. If you want the version that comes from inside the family, read how to write a best man speech for your brother. A friend can be just as close. The licence is different. You can say you chose him. A brother often cannot say that so cleanly, because the relationship arrived before the choice.

If you met as adults

Say so. Guests relax when you do not pretend to have known him at six. "I've known Daniel for seven years, which in this room makes me the new boy" is a stronger opening than a vague "we've been mates for ages". You will not have the school stories. You will have the ordinary evidence of adult friendship: who texts back, who drives, who stayed when it was dull. That evidence is enough.

There is already a short worked example of this on our examples page, example 4: a friend you met as an adult. Use that if you want the shape in miniature. The full example later on this page is the other case, two people who have known each other since they were children. Do not try to merge them into one speech that claims both kinds of memory.

Where the stories actually are

Friendship speeches fail when the best man tries to remember everything alone, the week before. Other people hold the useful details. Ask them while there is still time to cut.

  • His family. A parent or sibling will remember the version of a story you have smoothed. Ask for one memory, not "any stories?". Tell them the speech is kind, so they do not hand you a roast.
  • The old group. One message in the chat: "I need a specific thing he did, not a compliment." You are looking for an object, a place, a sentence.
  • His partner, carefully. You can ask what she loves about him, or a small habit she teases. Do not ask her to write your speech, and do not spoil a surprise she is keeping.
  • Your own phone. Old photos and the messages you actually sent each other are better than a reconstructed legend. A screenshot you would be happy to read aloud is a candidate. A screenshot you would hide is not.

Write ten fragments down before you pick. Then keep two. The best man speech template is the right place to drop them so the speech does not become a list of memories with no toast at the end. Opening lines help with the first twenty seconds, which is where a friend speech often wanders into "how do I even begin".

When you do not know the partner well

This is common, and it is not a moral failure. He may live in another city. You may have met his partner properly twice. The mistake is pretending otherwise. A paragraph of invented praise sounds like a paragraph of invented praise. His partner can hear it, and so can anyone who has watched the two of you be slightly formal with each other at dinners.

Say what you have really seen.

  • How he talks about them when they are not in the room.
  • One practical thing you noticed: he leaves on time, he is calmer, he mentions a plan that has both of them in it.
  • A welcome that does not fake intimacy. "I don't know you the way he does. I'm glad I'm going to."

That third line is often the most honest one in the speech, and guests trust the rest of it because of it. You can add a compliment you mean, even a small one: they were kind to his nervous friend, they laughed at his bad story, they made a Sunday feel easy. If you cannot think of a small true thing, you have not looked. You do not need a grand theory of their relationship.

Offer the friendship, do not hand it over like a resignation. "Look after him" can sound as if you are the previous owner. "There's a seat for you in the way we already do things" is warmer, if you mean it. If the partner is building a life that will not include every Sunday at the pub, say you are glad, and mean that too.

How to keep in-jokes out

An in-joke is a laugh that costs the rest of the room their place in the speech. You will feel the pull, because the in-joke is the proof of closeness. The proof does not need to be told in code.

Translate it. If the joke is a nickname, either explain it in one line or cut it. If the joke is a night out, take the character point and leave the night out. "He once talked a bouncer into letting us back in because he had left his coat" might be tellable. "You remember the thing with the coat" is not a sentence for a wedding. If only four people would laugh, it belongs in the pub afterwards.

Read the draft once and circle every name the average guest will not know. Each circled name needs a job, or it goes. The same test is in what not to say: if it only works for the group chat, it does not work at the top table.

Lines you can adapt

These are shapes, not lines to read unchanged. Put his name, and a detail that is his, into them.

  • Childhood friend, opening. "I'm Harry. I've known Daniel since we were nine, which means I remember the haircut and I have promised not to describe it."
  • Friend met later, opening. "We met at work, not at school. I'm not the keeper of his childhood. I'm the evidence that he is still choosing good people."
  • The loyal habit. "If you need him, he is already on the way. He will text to say he is on the way, and then he will text to say he is outside, and then he will come in with the thing you forgot you needed."
  • When you do not know the partner well. "Sophie, I can't tell your story. I can tell you what happened to his. He got lighter. He started making plans that were not just about next weekend."
  • A welcome. "You don't have to earn a place in this friendship. He already gave you one, and the rest of us are catching up."
  • The toast. "Please stand if you can. To Daniel and Sophie: the longest friendship in this speech, and the one that starts properly today."

A full example: lifelong best friends

Made-up names. Harry is the best man, Daniel is the groom, Sophie is his partner. They have known each other since primary school. Use this if that is your situation. If you met him as an adult, use example 4 instead of stretching this one.

I'm Harry. I've known Daniel since we were nine, which is long enough to have shared a paper round, a terrible band and a sofa that should have been taken to the tip in 2016. I am not his brother. I chose this, and so did he, which on the bad days is the better claim.

When we were eleven he made me captain of a football team that did not need a captain, because I was nervous and he thought a title would help. It did not help the football. It did tell me something I have used ever since. He notices who needs a role, and he hands them one, quietly, before they have to ask.

He is not tidy about it. He will be late, and he will bring the wrong cake, and he will have a theory about why the wrong cake is actually the right cake. But if something is wrong with you, he is the first person there and the last one to make it about himself. I broke my wrist on a Sunday in February and he sat in the waiting room doing a crossword badly, then drove me home the long way so I would not have to talk. I have never properly thanked him for the long way. This is as close as he is going to let me get.

Sophie, I have to be honest about the limits of my knowledge. I don't have years of Sunday lunches with you. I have the version of him that started leaving on time, and the version that talks about you as if the sentence was always going to end that way. The first time I met you, you asked me about the band, which means he had warned you, and you laughed in a way that included me. That was enough. You don't replace this friendship. You widen it.

Daniel has been my best friend for more than twenty years. Today he becomes your husband, which is the bigger job, and the one he has already started doing properly. Please raise your glasses. To Daniel and Sophie.

About five hundred words. One childhood detail, one adult detail, an honest limit about the partner, and a toast. If you need it shorter, drop the paper round and the cake and keep the waiting room. If you need the funny version of a friendship speech, the funny best man speech is a different build. Do not add jokes to this one until the welcome is something you would still say with the jokes taken out.

Frequently asked questions

How do you start a best man speech for your best friend?

Say your name and how you met, in one or two lines. A childhood friend can use the year or the place. A friend you met as an adult can say so plainly. Then go to one story, not a history of the friendship.

What if I do not know the bride or groom's partner well?

Say what you have actually seen: how your friend changed, one kind thing the partner did, and that you are glad. Do not invent a long portrait of someone you have met twice. Offer them a real welcome instead.

How is a best friend speech different from a brother speech?

A brother shares a family and a childhood whether he wants to or not. A best friend chose the friendship, and can say that. You also have less right to family stories. Ask before you use them.

What stories work in a best man speech for a friend?

One story that shows a habit, and one detail that shows he is loyal or kind. Skip in-jokes, nicknames the room does not know, and anything from a night out that needs explaining.

Can I be best man if we only met as adults?

Yes. Say how you met and what the years have been. Guests do not need a school story. They need to believe you know him. There is a shorter worked example of that situation on our examples page.
