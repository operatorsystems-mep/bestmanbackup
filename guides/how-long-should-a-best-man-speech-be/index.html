BEST MAN SPEECH LENGTH

How long should a best man speech be? Minutes, words and timings

Usually 3 to 5 minutes (390 to 650 words), or 5 to 7 if you are the main UK speaker.

Updated 27 November 2026

THE SHORT ANSWER

Usually 3 to 5 minutes (390 to 650 words), or 5 to 7 minutes for a main UK speaker.

  • Typical speech: 3–5 minutes, about 390–650 words
  • Main UK speaker: 5–7 minutes is fine
  • US reception toast: 3–5 minutes
  • Short speech or toast: 2–3 minutes (see a short best man speech example)
  • Joint best men: 7–10 minutes combined
  • Absolute ceiling: 10 minutes, about 1,300 words

Where your speech sits among the others is in wedding speech order. In a six-minute speech, that is about 45 seconds to open, 30 seconds of thanks, a minute and a half for each of two stories, just over a minute on the couple and 30 seconds to close.

Which length to pick

The right best man speech length is the one the room can stand, not the longest you can write.

Why the length changes with the room

Three to five minutes is where most best man speeches land, and it is enough for one proper story and a sincere turn. At a traditional UK wedding you often speak last, after the father of the bride and the groom, as Debrett's sets out. If you are that main speaker, five to seven minutes gives you a second story. Past ten, which is the ceiling BBC Radio 4 gives, the room drifts. The order of those speeches is in wedding speech order.

How to work out how long your speech is

Word counts are only a starting point, because people speak at different speeds. Guides put spoken pace at anything from about 100 to 150 words a minute, and around 130 is the figure most often used. So time yourself.

What changes the right length

The number of speeches

If there are four or five speeches, aim for the shorter end. If yours is the only speech after the groom's, six or seven minutes is comfortable.

Joint best men

Two best men shouldn't mean double the length. Keep a joint speech to one shared slot rather than two full speeches. See our joint best man speech guide.

The couple's wishes

How to cut a speech that's too long

  1. Thank-yous the groom will make. Keep yours to the bridesmaids and anything the couple asked for.
  2. Stories that need a long explanation. If the set-up takes longer than the payoff, cut it.
  3. Duplicate stories. If two stories make the same point, keep the better one.
  4. Openers you don't need. "Good evening, ladies and gentlemen, family and friends, honoured guests..." can just be "Good evening, everyone."
  5. Asides and tangents. For each paragraph, ask whether the speech needs it.

If you are rewriting rather than trimming line by line, drop the draft into the speech template and rebuild only the sections you need.

How to fill a speech that's too short

If you can't get past three minutes, don't pad it with jokes from the internet:

  • Ask his family and friends for one more story.
  • Add a section about the couple: how they met, and what you noticed when he met his partner.
  • Add one more specific detail to your best story.
  • Say what the friendship means to you, briefly and sincerely.

Signs on the day that you're running long

  • People start chatting at the back.
  • Waiting staff hover with plates.
  • You catch yourself skipping lines to speed up.

If that happens, skip straight to the couple and the toast. That's a good reason to write the speech in clear sections.

Practise with the clock

Do at least two fully timed run-throughs in the final week, standing up, from your cue cards. Nerves usually speed people up, so practise slightly slower than feels natural. Our nerves guide has more on pacing.

Frequently asked questions

How long should a best man speech be?

Most best man speeches work best at three to five minutes, roughly 390 to 650 words. If you are the main speaker at a UK wedding, five to seven minutes is fine, and ten is the ceiling. US toasts are usually three to five. If the couple asked for brief, see a short best man speech example.

How many words is a best man speech?

At around 130 words a minute, three to five minutes is about 390 to 650 words, which is where most speeches land. Five to seven minutes is about 650 to 910 words, for a main UK speaker. Ten minutes, about 1,300 words, is the ceiling.

How many words is a five-minute speech?

About 650 words at around 130 words a minute. People speak at anything from about 100 to 150 words a minute, so time a read-through out loud rather than relying on the word count.

How long is a best man speech in the US?

Usually three to five minutes. The Knot suggests three to five, and Brides treats five as the absolute maximum, because the US best man usually gives a toast during the reception.

How long should each part of a best man speech be?

For a six-minute speech: about 45 seconds for your opening, 30 seconds of thanks, 1½ minutes for each of two stories, 1¼ minutes on the couple and 30 seconds to close and toast.

How do I time my best man speech?

Stand up and read it aloud from your cards at the pace you'd use for a room, pause about two seconds where you expect laughs, and time it on your phone. Then allow extra for laughter and the toast.

What if my best man speech is too long?

Cut it. Trim the first draft. Drop thank-yous the groom will make, stories that need a long set-up, duplicate stories and long openers.

How long should a joint best man speech be?

Two best men shouldn't mean double the length. Aim for about seven to ten minutes combined, with ten as a sensible limit. See the joint best man speech.

How long should a best man toast be?

A closing toast is a few lines. If the toast is the whole speech, it is usually three to five minutes. See the best man toast.

I'm best man in a few weeks and haven't written anything. How long does my speech have to be?

Aim for three to five minutes if you are starting from nothing. Five to seven is fine if you are the main UK speaker and the stories are already there. A few weeks is enough: collect stories this week, draft next week, then cut and rehearse.

Is a three-minute best man speech too short?

No. Three to five minutes is where most best man speeches work best. A UK room can take five to seven if you are the main speaker. If you have been asked to keep it short, two to three minutes is a fair brief.

How long should the opening of a best man speech be?

About 45 seconds, roughly 100 words: say who you are, thank the hosts if needed, then move on. Keep jokes short. See best man speech opening lines for examples that fit that window.

Is a ten-minute best man speech too long?

Yes. Ten minutes is the ceiling most experts give, including BBC Radio 4. Stop well before it. Three to five minutes is typical, and five to seven is enough when you are the main UK speaker.
