BEST MAN SPEECH LENGTH

How long should a best man speech be? 5–7 minutes

THE SHORT ANSWER

A best man speech should be five to seven minutes long at a UK wedding. Aim for about six minutes, roughly 780 words, and never go past ten. At around 130 words a minute that's 650 to 900 words. US reception toasts usually run shorter, at three to five minutes.

  • Standard UK speech: 5–7 minutes (aim for about 6), 650–900 words
  • US reception toast: 3–5 minutes
  • Short speech or toast: 2–3 minutes
  • Joint best men: 7–10 minutes combined
  • Absolute ceiling: 10 minutes, about 1,300 words

In a six-minute speech, that's about 45 seconds for your opening, 30 seconds of thanks, 1½ minutes for each of two stories, 1¼ minutes on the couple and 30 seconds to close and toast (full breakdown below). Laughter adds time, so time a full read-through out loud.

Why five to seven minutes works

At a traditional UK wedding the best man speaks last, after the father of the bride and the groom, as Debrett's sets out. By then guests have been sitting for a while. Six minutes gives you room for two or three proper stories and a sincere section about the couple, and it finishes before attention drops.

How to work out how long your speech is

Word counts are only a starting point, because people speak at different speeds. Guides put spoken pace at anything from about 100 to 150 words a minute, and around 130 is the figure most often used. So time yourself.

  1. Print it or put it on cards. Read from whatever you'll use on the day.
  2. Stand up and read it aloud at the pace you'd use for a room. That's slower than conversation.
  3. Pause where you expect laughs. Count two seconds at each one.
  4. Time it with your phone, then allow extra for laughter and the toast.

If your timed read is already seven minutes, the real speech will probably run longer. Cut it. Hitched suggests cutting your first draft by about 20%, and that's a good rule of thumb.

What changes the right length

The number of speeches

If there are four or five speeches, aim for the shorter end. If yours is the only speech after the groom's, six or seven minutes is comfortable.

Joint best men

Two best men shouldn't mean double the length. Speechwriting guides suggest roughly six to eight or seven to eight minutes combined, with ten as a sensible limit. See our joint best man speech guide.

The couple's wishes

Ask them. If they say "keep it short", two to three minutes is a fair brief. Our short speech guide shows how to make that feel complete.

When the speeches happen

Speeches traditionally come after the meal, but Hitched notes it's increasingly popular to hold them before the meal or between courses. If you're speaking before people have eaten, keep it tighter.

US receptions

In the US the best man toast usually comes during the reception, often next to a maid of honour toast. Three to five minutes is the norm there. Our best man toast guide covers the US-style toast.

How to cut a speech that's too long

  1. Thank-yous the groom will make. Keep yours to the bridesmaids and anything the couple asked for.
  2. Stories that need a long explanation. If the set-up takes longer than the payoff, cut it.
  3. Duplicate stories. If two stories make the same point, keep the better one.
  4. Openers you don't need. "Good evening, ladies and gentlemen, family and friends, honoured guests..." can just be "Good evening, everyone."
  5. Asides and tangents. For each paragraph, ask whether the speech needs it.

How to fill a speech that's too short

If you can't get past three minutes, don't pad it with jokes from the internet:

  • Ask his family and friends for one more story.
  • Add a section about the couple: how they met, and what you noticed when he met his partner.
  • Add one more specific detail to your best story.
  • Say what the friendship means to you, briefly and sincerely.

Signs on the day that you're running long

  • People start chatting at the back.
  • Waiting staff hover with plates.
  • You catch yourself skipping lines to speed up.

If that happens, skip straight to the couple and the toast. That's a good reason to write the speech in clear sections.

Practise with the clock

Do at least two fully timed run-throughs in the final week, standing up, from your cue cards. Nerves usually speed people up, so practise slightly slower than feels natural. Our nerves guide has more on pacing.

Frequently asked questions

How long should a best man speech be?

For a UK wedding, five to seven minutes, about six being ideal, and never more than ten. US toasts tend to be three to five minutes.

How many words is a best man speech?

At around 130 words a minute, a five- to seven-minute speech is roughly 650 to 900 words. Ten minutes, about 1,300 words, is the ceiling most experts give.

How many words is a five-minute speech?

About 650 words at around 130 words a minute. People speak at anything from about 100 to 150 words a minute, so time a read-through out loud rather than relying on the word count.

How long is a best man speech in the US?

Usually three to five minutes. The Knot suggests three to five, and Brides treats five as the absolute maximum, because the US best man usually gives a toast during the reception.

How long should each part of a best man speech be?

For a six-minute speech: about 45 seconds for your opening, 30 seconds of thanks, 1½ minutes for each of two stories, 1¼ minutes on the couple and 30 seconds to close and toast.

How do I time my best man speech?

Stand up and read it aloud from your cards at the pace you'd use for a room, pause about two seconds where you expect laughs, and time it on your phone. Then allow extra for laughter and the toast.

What if my best man speech is too long?

Cut it. Hitched suggests trimming your first draft by about 20%. Drop thank-yous the groom will make, stories that need a long set-up, duplicate stories and long openers.

How long should a joint best man speech be?

Two best men shouldn't mean double the length. Aim for about seven to ten minutes combined, with ten as a sensible limit.

I'm best man in a few weeks and haven't written anything. How long does my speech have to be?

Aim for about six minutes, roughly 780 words. A few weeks is enough: collect stories this week, draft next week, then cut and rehearse.

Is a three-minute best man speech too short?

No, especially if there are several speeches or the couple asked for brief. US guides treat three minutes as normal, though a UK room usually expects a bit more. If you've been asked to keep it short, two to three minutes is a fair brief.

Is a ten-minute best man speech too long?

Ten minutes is the maximum most experts give. For a single best man, five to seven minutes is far safer.
