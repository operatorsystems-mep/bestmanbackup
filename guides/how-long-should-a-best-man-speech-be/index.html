BEST MAN SPEECH LENGTH

How long should a best man speech be?

THE SHORT ANSWER

Aim for about three to five minutes for most weddings, let it stretch to seven only if the couple are happy, and treat ten minutes as the outer limit. At a relaxed speaking pace that's very roughly 400 to 650 written words, but laughter and pauses add time, so always time a full read-through out loud rather than relying on a word count.

The short version

SpeechSpoken timeRough script lengthGood for
Short1–2 minutes150–300 wordsJoint speeches, small weddings, couples who asked for brief
Standard3–5 minutes400–650 wordsMost weddings
Longer6–8 minutes750–1,000 wordsOnly if the couple want it and you're a confident speaker

The word counts are a guide, not a rule. People speak at different speeds, and a speech packed with laughs runs longer than the same number of words read straight.

Why three to five minutes works

The best man often speaks last at UK weddings, after the father of the bride and the groom. By then guests have been sitting for a while, they've eaten, and the bar is calling. Three to five minutes is long enough to tell two or three proper stories and say something heartfelt about the couple, and short enough that nobody checks their watch.

Short and good beats long and good. Nobody has ever complained that a funny, warm best man speech finished too soon.

How to work out how long your speech is

Word counts only get you so far. The reliable method is to time yourself.

  1. Print it or put it on cards. Read from what you'll use on the day.
  2. Stand up and read it aloud at the pace you'd use for a room. Slower than conversation.
  3. Pause where you expect laughs. Count two seconds at each one.
  4. Time it with your phone. Then add about a minute for the room: laughs, a toast, a heckle from Uncle Mike.

If your timed read is already six minutes, the real speech will probably run to seven or more. Cut it.

A rough rule for word counts

Many people speak at somewhere around 120 to 150 words a minute when they're presenting, but nerves push people faster and laughter slows the overall speech down. Use the timed read, not the maths.

What changes the right length

The number of speeches

If there are four or five speeches, keep yours to the shorter end. If you're the only speech after the groom, you have a bit more room.

Joint best men

Two best men shouldn't mean double the length. Aim for the same total as one speech, perhaps a minute or two longer. Our joint best man speech guide covers how to split it.

The couple's wishes

Ask them. Some couples specifically want short speeches. If they say "two minutes, please", that's your brief. Our short speech guide shows how to make two minutes feel complete.

The room and the timing

A speech before the meal with hungry guests needs to be tighter than one after dessert. Outdoor or noisy venues also favour shorter speeches, because people tire of straining to hear.

US receptions

In the US the best man toast is often given during the reception, and the expectation is usually a little shorter, with the maid of honour also speaking. Two to four minutes is a common target. Our best man toast guide covers the US-style toast.

How to split your time

A clear plan for each section stops any one part swelling. For a four-minute speech:

SectionTimeRoughly
Opening line and who you are30 seconds2–4 sentences
Any thank-yous the couple asked for15 seconds1–2 sentences
First story1 minuteA short paragraph with a set-up, a turn and a point
Second story1 minuteSame again, ideally on the same theme
The couple45 secondsHow they met, what changed, something about the partner
Close and toast30 secondsOne sincere line, then both names

If you're aiming for five minutes, give the stories a little more room. If you need three, drop the second story.

How to cut a speech that's too long

Every first draft runs long. Here's the order to cut in:

  1. Thank-yous the groom will make. One line at most.
  2. Stories that need a long explanation. If the set-up takes longer than the payoff, it goes.
  3. Duplicate stories. If two stories make the same point, keep the better one.
  4. Openers you don't need. "Good evening, ladies and gentlemen, family and friends, honoured guests..." becomes "Good evening, everyone."
  5. Asides and tangents. Read each paragraph and ask, does the speech need it?

Then read it aloud again and time it. Cutting is where good speeches get made.

How to fill a speech that's too short

If you're struggling to reach three minutes, don't pad with jokes from the internet. Instead:

  • Ask his family and friends for one more story.
  • Add a section about the couple: how they met, what you noticed when he met them.
  • Expand your best story with one more specific detail.
  • Say what the friendship means to you, briefly and sincerely.

The how to write a best man speech guide has prompts for finding more material.

Signs your speech is too long on the day

  • People start chatting at the back.
  • Waiting staff hover with plates.
  • You catch yourself skipping lines to speed up.

If that happens, trust your cue cards: skip to your final story or straight to the couple and the toast. That's another reason to write your speech in clear sections, so you know what you can drop.

Practise with the clock

Do at least two fully timed run-throughs in the final week, standing up, from your cue cards. If you're nervous, you'll probably speed up on the day, so practise slightly slower than feels natural. Our nerves guide has more on pacing.

Frequently asked questions

How long should a best man speech be?

About three to five minutes suits most weddings. Keep it under ten minutes, and shorter if there are several speeches or the couple ask for brief.

How many words is a five-minute best man speech?

Very roughly 550 to 650 words, but speaking speeds vary and laughter adds time. Time a full read-through out loud to be sure.

Is a two-minute best man speech too short?

No. A well-written two-minute speech with an opening laugh, one good story, a line about the couple and a toast is perfectly respectable, especially if there are several speeches.

Is a ten-minute best man speech too long?

For most rooms, yes. Ten minutes should be the absolute maximum, and only if the couple are happy and the material is strong.
