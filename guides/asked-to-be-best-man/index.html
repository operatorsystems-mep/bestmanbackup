Asked to be best man: what to do first

Updated 18 November 2026

About these guides: written by Best Man Backup, an independent site for best men only.

THE SHORT ANSWER

Say thank you, then ask what he actually wants from you. In the first week, get the date, budget boundaries, guest-list rules for the stag or bachelor party, and whether you are holding the rings and giving a speech. Write those answers down. Everything else can wait a fortnight.

The first conversation

Reddit threads from first-time best men agree on the useful opener: “I’d be honoured — what do you want me to own?” Support looks different at every wedding. One groom wants a full stag weekend; another wants a quiet pub lunch and a short toast.

  • Wedding date and ceremony location
  • Whether there is a stag do / bachelor party, and any hard noes
  • Who else is in the wedding party
  • Suit hire vs buy, and who pays
  • Speech expected? Approximate length?
  • Rings — you or someone else?

When to say no — or renegotiate

If you cannot travel, cannot afford the proposed stag, or cannot speak in public even with help, say so early. A partial yes (“I can do the day and the speech, not a destination stag”) is better than a silent spiral. Couples would rather adjust than be surprised.

Where to start on the speech

Do not write the final draft this month. Collect stories. When you are ready, use how to write a best man speech and the Speech Kit first — most new best men underestimate the toast and overestimate the admin.

A simple yes script

“I’d be honoured. Can we grab half an hour this week so I know what you want me to take on?” That sentence does two jobs: it accepts with warmth, and it sets a planning conversation so you are not guessing for six months.

If you need time to check leave, money or a conflicting wedding, say so. “I want to say yes properly — can I confirm by Friday after I check work leave?” is adult, not cold.

Systems that stop overwhelm

  • One note file or shared doc titled with the couple’s names.
  • One group chat for the stag or bachelor party attendees.
  • One calendar with stag date, suit fitting, rehearsal, wedding.
  • One money record for deposits.

You do not need wedding-planner software. You need fewer places for facts to hide. When someone asks “what’s the plan?”, you should be able to answer from one screen.

Emotional labour is part of the job

Best man is not only logistics. Check in on him in the final month. Ask how he is sleeping. Offer specific help (“I can chase the ushers about shoes”) rather than “let me know if you need anything”. Forum advice from first-time best men often underplays this part; duty checklists put support next to the stag and the speech for a reason.

If your own life is heavy, be honest about bandwidth early. A best man who disappears in month five is harder to replace than one who renegotiates in month one.

Mapping the year at a glance

Once you have the date, sketch seasons: when the stag must happen so people can book leave; when suits need ordering; when the speech draft should exist; when the week-before checklist starts. You do not need perfect dates on day one. You need empty boxes that will not be forgotten.

Share a lightweight version with the groom so expectations stay aligned. If he wants a destination stag and half the invitees have toddlers and tiny budgets, surface that early with options, not with panic a month out.

Learning without drowning in tabs

Read three pages on this site and stop: the job overview, the timeline, and how to write the speech. Everything else can wait until a specific problem appears. Endless browsing feels like progress and produces nothing.

First-week message templates

To the groom: “Thinking about dates for a stag — any hard noes on city, spend or surprise level?” To the groomsmen: “I’ve been asked to coordinate. Reply with your rough budget ceiling and blocked weekends before we pick a place.” Clear questions beat long essays.

If nobody replies, chase once with a deadline. Admin silence is how best men end up paying deposits alone.

Saying yes when you live far away

Distance is workable if you name it early. You can still lead speech and day-of cues while a local groomsman handles vendor pickups. Say that split out loud so nobody assumes you will drive across the country for every fitting.

Budget travel for the stag and the wedding weekend separately. If you can only afford one big trip, tell the groom before deposits land. Honesty here protects the friendship more than silent overstretch.

What success looks like

Success is a calm groom, a speech that sounds like you, and a day where rings, timing and the toast all happen without drama. It is not a perfect Instagram grid. When pressure rises, return to those three outcomes and cut anything that does not serve them.

When family expects a different choice

Sometimes a parent assumed a brother would be asked. Stay out of that politics unless the groom asks you to help. Your job is to support him, not to win an argument with relatives. A short, warm line in the speech about family can help the room settle without naming the tension.

Frequently asked questions

What do I say when someone asks me to be best man?

Thank them, then ask what they want you to take ownership of. Clarify stag, speech, suit and rings early.

Can I say no?

Yes, if you truly cannot do the job. Be honest early and help them think about who else could step in.

What is the first thing I should organise?

The date in your calendar and a written list of expectations. The stag comes after budget filters are clear.

Do I have to plan a stag do?

Only if the groom wants one. Some couples skip it or keep it tiny.

When should I start the speech?

Collect notes in the first month; draft properly in the final six to eight weeks unless the wedding is sooner.
