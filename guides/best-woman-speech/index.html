THE SPEECH

Best woman speech: how to write it, with example lines

THE SHORT ANSWER

Write it like any best man speech: introduce yourself and how you know the groom, thank the right people, tell two or three warm stories about him, say why the couple work, and finish with a toast. Acknowledge being the best woman once, lightly, in your opening, then let the friendship carry it. Aim for five to seven minutes.

Is a best woman speech any different?

Not really. A best woman (sometimes called a best person, or a "best man" who happens to be a woman) does the same job as any best man: support the groom, look after the rings if asked, and give a speech that makes the room laugh, then makes it feel something, then raises a glass. The structure, the length and the rules are the same.

What's different is usually just the opening. Some guests won't expect a woman at the microphone, so most best women acknowledge it once, lightly, and move on. After that, it's your friendship with the groom that carries the speech, not your title.

How long it should be

Aim for five to seven minutes, which is roughly 600 to 900 words spoken at a relaxed pace. That's long enough for two or three good stories and a proper tribute, and short enough that nobody checks their phone. Our guide to how long a best man speech should be breaks the timing down, and if you'd rather keep it brief there's a short speech guide too.

Opening lines for a best woman

Pick one, make it sound like you, and keep it to a single line. The goal is to acknowledge the moment and get a warm laugh, not to make the speech about the title.

  • "Good afternoon, everyone. I'm [name], and yes, I'm the best man. [Groom] said he wanted someone organised, reliable and better at speeches than him. I'm still not sure why he picked me."
  • "Hello, I'm [name]. I've known [groom] for [number] years, which is why I'm up here and not in a bridesmaid's dress."
  • "Some of you may have been expecting a slightly taller, slightly hairier best man. Sorry. You've got me."
  • "I'm [name], [groom]'s best woman. When he asked me, I said yes before he'd finished the sentence. Then I asked what it involved. Then I asked if I could change my answer."
  • "Good evening. For those who don't know me, I'm [name], and I've been [groom]'s best friend since [place or year]. The title says best man. The job description says 'someone who knows where the bodies are buried'."

Stories that work

The best stories show the groom's character: loyalty, kindness, terrible decisions made with total confidence. As his best woman you often have an angle nobody else in the room has, so use it.

  • How you met. School, work, a shared flat, a five-a-side team. Keep it to the moment that made you friends.
  • The friend who shows up. One specific time he was there for you. This is the line that makes people put their drinks down.
  • The view from the outside. What you noticed when he met his partner, before he admitted anything.
  • A running joke, explained in one line. If it needs more than a sentence of set-up, cut it.

If you're stuck for material, our three prompts to start your speech are a quick way to get a first page down.

What to avoid

  • Making the whole speech about being a woman. One line is charming. Five is a stand-up set.
  • Ex-partners, and anything that would land badly with grandparents in the room.
  • Hinting that you and the groom were ever more than friends, even as a joke. It's the one gag that reliably goes wrong at a best woman speech.
  • In-jokes the room can't follow. If you need to explain it, it isn't working.
  • Reading the speech word for word from your phone. Use cue cards and look up.

Ending and the toast

Finish by turning to the couple. Say one sincere thing about why they're right for each other, then ask everyone to stand: "Please raise your glasses to [name] and [name]." Our guide to how to end a best man speech has more closing lines, and there are ready-made toasts in our toast examples.

Where the best woman speaks

At a traditional UK wedding the best man speaks last, after the father of the bride and the groom (Debrett's). The best woman takes that same slot. In the US the best man's toast often comes earlier, sometimes alongside the maid of honour's. Many couples change the order, so check the running order with them before the day.

Practise, then put it on cards

Read it out loud at least three times and time it. Cut anything you stumble on. Then move it onto a few cue cards with one line per point, and rehearse from the cards rather than the script. If nerves are a worry, our guide to handling speech nerves has practical tips for the day.

Frequently asked questions

What is a best woman?

A best woman is a woman who takes the best man role for the groom. She does the same jobs, including the speech, and may be called best woman, best person or simply best man.

How long should a best woman speech be?

Five to seven minutes is a good target, roughly 600 to 900 words spoken at a relaxed pace.

Should a best woman mention that she is a woman in the speech?

Many do, once and lightly in the opening, because some guests won't expect it. After that, focus on the groom and the couple.

When does the best woman give her speech?

She takes the best man's slot. At a traditional UK wedding that is last, after the father of the bride and the groom. Couples often change the order, so check with them.

Can I use a best man speech template for a best woman speech?

Yes. The structure is the same. Swap in your own opening line and stories, and leave out anything that only works coming from a man.
