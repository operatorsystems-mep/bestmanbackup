WRITING THE SPEECH

Best man speech for a colleague: from work friend to wedding toast

Updated 17 November 2026

About these guides: written by Best Man Backup, an independent site for best men only.

THE SHORT ANSWER

You do not need childhood stories. Open with how you met at work in one sentence, tell one work story that shows character when nobody is scoring points, add one story or detail that includes his partner, then toast both names. Avoid org-chart jokes, Slack-only punchlines and anything that sounds like a performance review.

The job of a colleague speech

Families never saw you at school. They saw job titles. Your minutes have to turn “we work together” into “this is who he is when the work is gone.”

  • One sentence for the origin, then leave the org chart.
  • One scene that proves character.
  • One beat that includes the partner so it is a wedding speech, not a leaving do.

Stories that work from work

  • The night he stayed so someone else could leave.
  • How he talked about his partner before you had met them.
  • The first time the friendship left the building — a dinner, a lift home, a weekend.
  • A calm save on a bad day, told without naming clients or confidential detail.

Weak material: job titles as comedy, jargon, roast-the-boss bits, and anything that makes his partner feel like a visitor to an office club.

Structure and timing

Three to five minutes. If you have only known him a few years, two to three is honest. Use the speech template and cut thank-you lists the groom will already cover.

Opening lines

  • “I’m Ade. I met Chris at work five years ago, which makes me the shortest-serving person on this table, so I asked around and then decided to tell you my story instead.”
  • “I’m Sam. I first knew Tom as the person who still asked how your weekend was when the week had already gone sideways.”
  • “Hello. I’m Priya. We met on a project that should have broken us and somehow made us friends instead.”

Worked example

Good evening. I’m Ade. I met Chris at work five years ago. My first week, I got something badly wrong — the sort of wrong you lie awake about. The next morning Chris had already fixed it in a way that made it look like it had never happened. He never mentioned it. I only know because somebody else told me months later.

That is a particular kind of person: not the one who helps you and makes sure you know, the one who helps you and walks off. Leah, I have watched him talk about you at work for years, in the way people do when they think they are being subtle and are not. The whole floor knew he was going to propose about a year before he did.

I have only known Chris five years. It was enough. Please raise your glasses — to Chris and Leah.

Frequently asked questions

Can a work friend be best man?

Yes. The speech just has to prove the friendship with scenes, not with years on a CV.

What if I do not know the partner well?

Ask one question before the day, then say one true thing you have observed. Do not pretend to a deep history.

Are office jokes OK?

Only if half the room will get them without a glossary. When in doubt, cut them.

How long should a colleague best man speech be?

Three to five minutes, or two to three if the friendship is newer.

Should I mention the company?

Once, lightly, is enough. The speech is about him, not the brand.
