Dad as best man: how to write the speech

Father and best man: proud, short, and no embarrassing baby stories.

Updated 17 November 2026

THE SHORT ANSWER

Be a father who happens to be best man, not a mate pretending to be twenty-five. Open with who you are, tell one specific story that shows who your son is now, welcome his partner by name, then toast the couple. Skip nappy jokes, CV lists and anything that makes the day about your parenting.

What the room expects

You have more history than anyone else. That is an advantage only if you filter it. One story that reveals character beats a childhood montage. Three to five minutes is enough; length rules are the same as any best man speech.

Structure

  1. Who you are — dad and best man, said once, lightly.
  2. One story about him as an adult, or a childhood moment that clearly became a trait.
  3. What you have noticed since his partner arrived.
  4. A welcome to them (and their family if you wish).
  5. The toast.

If you are also speaking as father of the groom in some US orders, confirm with the couple so you do not deliver two overlapping speeches. See wedding speech order.

What to avoid

  • Nappies, dating history and exes.
  • “My little boy” for longer than half a line.
  • A résumé of grades, jobs and trophies.
  • Marital advice from the microphone.
  • Anything that upstages the couple.

Opening lines

  • “I’ve known the groom longer than anyone in this room, which mostly means I’ve had more time to be proud and somehow never ran out.”
  • “I’m David, Tom’s dad, and today I’m also his best man. I checked the parenting books. They skipped this chapter.”
  • “Tom asked me to be best man. I said yes before he could change his mind. Then I asked what the speech rules were. He said ‘don’t cry’. We will see.”

Worked example

I’m David, Tom’s dad, and his best man today. I used to think my job was to teach him how to be a man. Somewhere along the way he started teaching me — mostly about showing up without making a fuss.

When his sister’s car broke down on a freezing night, he drove four hours and did not think twice. He still will not let me tell that story as if it were heroic. So I will only say this: that is who he is when nobody is keeping score.

Jess, since you arrived he has been earlier to family lunches and kinder in the group chat, which I did not think was a metric, and yet here we are. You make him look up. We are glad you are here.

Please raise your glasses. To Tom and Jess.

Frequently asked questions

Can a dad be best man?

Yes. It is increasingly common. Confirm the speech slot so you are not also expected to give a separate father-of-the-groom speech unless the couple want both.

How long should a dad-as-best-man speech be?

Three to five minutes, same as a standard best man speech.

Should I talk about his childhood?

One moment that explains who he is now can work. A long childhood reel usually does not.

What if I get emotional?

Practise the hard lines standing up. Pause, breathe, continue. Cue cards help. See the nerves guide.

How do I include his partner?

Say their name, what you have noticed, and a clear welcome. Do not make them a footnote before the toast.
