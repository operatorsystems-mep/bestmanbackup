Cheap stag do ideas: low-cost UK plans that still work

Low cost isn't low effort. UK plans that feel deliberate, not cheap.

THE SHORT ANSWER

Pick a format first: local day, one night, or a self-catered weekend. Book one modest paid activity if you want a headline, then fill the rest with a pub walk, park sport, home cooking and games. Set the per-head number before you book, and cover the groom’s share in that number. Prices below are examples from public UK activity listings — always re-check before you pay.

Budget rules before ideas

Cheap fails when you plan the dream weekend first. Agree a per-head figure based on the least well-off person invited, then choose ideas that fit. How to collect money and cover the groom is in stag do budget. Full planning order is in how to plan a stag do.

  • Prefer UK over flights when money is tight.
  • One night beats two if travel is the cost.
  • Avoid peak football, race and bank holiday weekends where rooms jump.
  • Cook some meals; do not buy every round as a group surprise.

Day plans (often under a pub-lunch budget)

  • Park football, cricket or rounders, then a reserved pub table.
  • Home or garden BBQ with a simple cook rota.
  • Coast or reservoir day with a shared picnic.
  • Five-a-side hire for an hour, then board games at someone’s flat.
  • DIY tasting at home: each person brings one bottle and a note.

Paid activities that are often lower cost

Public UK activity pages commonly list entry-level stag activities from roughly the mid-teens to about £35 per person for things like paintball, smartphone pub hunts or sports-bar meals — for example Funktion Events’ cheap-activity grid shows several options under £35pp at last check. Treat those figures as examples, not a promise; operators change prices.

  • Paintball or airsoft off-peak.
  • Escape room + supermarket lunch.
  • Axe throwing daytime slots.
  • Bowling or crazy golf before the evening.

Night and weekend on a budget

  • Self-led pub crawl with three stops and a walking route, not a party bus.
  • Shared house or apartment with a kitchen instead of separate hotel rooms, if the listing allows stag groups — message hosts first.
  • Poker night with a low buy-in and a silly trophy.
  • Games from our stag do games list so the night is not only drinking.
  • For groups avoiding alcohol as the main plan, see non-drinking stag do ideas.

The Guardian’s 2025 cost-cutting advice for hen and stag trips also points to shorter celebrations, home hosting and shared houses as practical levers — useful framing even when your numbers differ.

Frequently asked questions

What is a good cheap stag do idea in the UK?

A local day with one modest activity, a cooked meal and a short pub plan is the most reliable low-cost format.

How do I keep a stag do cheap without excluding people?

Set the per-head budget around the least well-off guest, keep travel simple, and make expensive extras optional.

Does cheap mean no activity?

No. Park sport, a low-cost paid session and home games all count if the groom is the focus.

Should the groom still be covered?

Traditionally yes. Build his share into the per-head price from the start.

Where do games fit?
