WRITING THE SPEECH

Heartfelt best man speech: sincere without the cringe

Updated 17 November 2026

About these guides: written by Best Man Backup, an independent site for best men only.

THE SHORT ANSWER

Build the speech around one true story that shows who he is, say plainly what you admire, speak to his partner by name, then toast. You can still be funny — put the laughs in the setup, not in the line you care about. Practise the emotional sentences standing up so a pause does not become a stop.

Heartfelt is not humourless

A sincere speech still needs shape and breath. The difference from a roast is where the weight sits: character and the couple at the centre, jokes as seasoning. If you want laugh-led structure, use our funny best man speech guide instead and steal only the testing habits.

Find the honest core

  • Write the line you would be embarrassed to say, then edit it until it sounds like you.
  • Pick one shared experience that proves the point.
  • Cut adjectives that could sit in a performance review — loyal, driven, solid — unless a scene earns them.

Structure

PartJob
OpenWho you are; one light line
StoryOne scene, specific detail, what it shows
PartnerWhat changed; why they work
Direct lineOptional: one sentence to him
ToastBoth names

Practising emotion

If a sentence catches your throat, that is the one to rehearse most. Breathe before it. A pause is fine. Stopping completely is harder. Cue cards with the exact wording of the sincere lines help — see cue cards and nerves.

Worked example

I’m Pete. I’ve been Jack’s closest friend since we were twenty-two and both pretending we knew what we were doing. I kept writing versions of this speech that tried too hard. What I kept coming back to was simpler: I’m lucky to know him.

When Jack was in hospital a few years ago, I sat outside and thought about what I would want people to know if I ever had to say something formal. I would want them to know he is good when nobody is watching — in decisions that cost him something, and in how he treats people who cannot do anything for him.

Lydia, you have the best of him. We can see what you bring out, and it is what we hoped for. Jack — thank you. I mean that without a punchline behind it.

Please raise your glasses. To Jack and Lydia.

Frequently asked questions

How do you write a heartfelt best man speech?

Choose one true story, say what it shows, name the partner, and end on a clear toast. Practise the sincere lines aloud.

Can a heartfelt speech still be funny?

Yes. Keep humour in the setup and protect the line you mean.

How long should it be?

Three to five minutes is enough for one story and a proper tribute.

What if I cry?

Pause, breathe, continue from your card. The room is usually with you.

Should I say ‘I love you’ to the groom?

If it is true and sounds like you, yes. If it does not sound like you, say the specific thank-you instead.
