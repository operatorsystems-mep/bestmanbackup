WRITING THE SPEECH

Last-minute best man speech: write it tonight

Updated 17 November 2026

About these guides: written by Best Man Backup, an independent site for best men only.

THE SHORT ANSWER

Stop searching for the perfect joke. Spend about an hour: dump six memories, pick one safe story, write who you are, the story, one line about the partner, and a toast. Aim for two to four minutes. Read it aloud twice, put prompts on cards, and stop rewriting.

The one-hour plan

MinutesTask
0–5Write names, how you know him, target length, toast line
5–15List six memories; circle the safest clear story
15–35Write the draft out loud in your own words
35–45Cut setup; keep partner line and toast
45–60Two standing reads; move to cue cards

If you have a whole evening, add one trusted listener. If you only have thirty minutes, write the four-line minimum at the end of this page.

The structure that saves you

  1. Who you are — name and relationship, one smile.
  2. One story — setup, what he did, what it shows.
  3. The partner — one specific observation.
  4. The toast — both names, one wish, sit down.

Do not add a second story. Do not open with a dictionary definition. Do not warm up for two minutes. Full process when you have time: how to write a best man speech. Length help: short best man speech.

How to pick a safe story fast

  • Would his gran and her parents be fine hearing it?
  • Can half the room follow it without a back-story?
  • Does it show a trait, not just a funny accident?
  • Can you tell it in under a minute?

Stag do stories, exes and in-jokes almost always fail. See what not to say.

A worked last-minute example

About 280 words — roughly two minutes with pauses.

I’m Ellis. I’ve known Noah since a Sunday league side that lost most weeks, and he was the one who brought the oranges and never mentioned the score on the drive home. He still only texts the result if we won, which is a kind of optimism I have borrowed for years.

That is still him. He turns up, he stays, and he makes the people around him feel the day went fine even when the result says otherwise. Maya, you are the person he tells the truth to first, usually before he has told me. Since you arrived he has been steadier, and a bit kinder to the rest of us as a side effect.

Please raise your glasses. To Noah and Maya.

Four-line emergency version

If the wedding is tomorrow morning and you are staring at a blank note:

  1. I’m [name], [groom]’s best man / friend since [when].
  2. One detail that shows who he is.
  3. One sentence about [partner] and what you have noticed.
  4. To [name] and [name].

Read it twice. That is a toast-length speech and it is better than improvising risk.

Rehearse without burning the night

Two standing runs beat ten silent reads. Put the open and the toast on cards word for word. Everything else can be prompts. If nerves spike, use the nerves guide. The Speech Kit still helps under deadline because the workbook forces one story and cue cards.

Frequently asked questions

Can I write a best man speech the night before?

Yes. Keep it to one story, write the toast first, and rehearse out loud twice. Earlier is better, but a clear short speech beats a messy long one.

How long should a last-minute best man speech be?

Two to four minutes is safer than a five-to-seven-minute draft you cannot rehearse.

Should I memorise a last-minute speech?

No. Know the open and toast; use cue cards for the middle.

What if I cannot think of a funny story?

Use a kind, specific story instead. Warm and clear beats forced jokes under time pressure.

Is it OK to read it?

Reading from cards is fine. Reading from a glowing phone is harder on the room. Large type, glance, look up.
