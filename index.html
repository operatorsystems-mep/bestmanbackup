BEFORE YOU CHOOSE

A few good
questions.

Which kit should I choose?

Choose Duties for your role, wedding-day responsibilities and the stag do budget, or Speech for writing and delivery. The Best Man Bundle combines the Duties Kit, the Stag & Bachelor Party Kit and the Speech Kit. If you want games for the stag or bachelor night, the Playbook is a separate kit. You can start speech preparation while the duties and party plans are still underway.

What software will I need?

The editable documents are Word files, intended for a current desktop version of Microsoft Word. Layouts can vary in other editors; check your saved pages before printing. Read and print the PDF versions with a PDF reader. Microsoft Word licences are not included. Check each listing for the full file formats before buying.

Can I use it outside the UK or US?

The kits are in English, with A4 and US Letter print layouts. The planning approach can be adapted to your celebration, including an Australian bucks party. There are no translated editions or country-specific ceremony rules. Agree traditions, responsibilities and wording with the couple and their officiant.

Can I use the kits only on my phone?

Read the PDFs on a phone, or draft your speech in your own notes alongside the guide. Your notes do not sync with the templates. A computer is recommended for the Word documents. Paper worksheets and notes require manual totals. These are downloadable files, not a mobile app.

I already have a speech draft. Where do I start?

Use the Speech Kit’s editing and rehearsal guidance to refine your draft, then transfer short reminders to the cue cards. Keep your full script as a backup. You do not need to start again with story-finding prompts.

Are the PDFs fillable?

The PDFs are print layouts. Use the editable files to type your details, or print the worksheets and write by hand.

Is anything posted to me?

These are digital downloads. Print the pages you need at home or through your preferred print service.

Will you write my speech or organise the party?

You personalise and use the kits yourself. Custom speech writing, coaching, bookings and event management are not included.

How do I get help with a download?

Download through Etsy in a browser, extract the ZIP and open Start Here. If you need help, message Best Man Backup through your Etsy order with the filename, device and application you’re using.
