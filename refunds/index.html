Refunds

Last updated: 28 November 2026

The kits are digital files. Nothing is posted. Email hello@bestmanbackup.com and we will sort a refund with you.

The 14-day right to cancel

Under the Consumer Contracts (Information, Cancellation and Additional Charges) Regulations 2013, if you buy as a consumer you normally have 14 days to cancel a distance contract. The 14 days start the day after you pay.

When that right is lost for a download

For digital content that is not supplied on a disc or other physical item, that cancellation right is lost once the download starts, but only if, before the download starts, you have done both of these:

  • expressly agreed that we can supply the files during the 14-day cancellation period, and
  • acknowledged that you lose the right to cancel once the download starts.

We also have to confirm that agreement and that acknowledgement on a durable medium, such as the receipt email.

Our checkout is a Stripe payment page. It does not yet ask for that separate agreement. Because of that, we do not treat the statutory 14-day right as lost just because the download has started.

Our no-quibble refund

On top of that right, you can email hello@bestmanbackup.com within 14 days of payment and ask for a refund. We refund you in full, with no quibble, even if you have already downloaded the files. Tell us the email address you paid with.

The refund goes back to the same card, through Stripe.

After 14 days, email us anyway. We will help where we reasonably can, for example if a file will not open.

Free cards

The free cards are free, so there is nothing to refund. You can ask us to delete the email address you used. That is covered in the privacy notice.

You’ve got his back. We’ve got yours.
