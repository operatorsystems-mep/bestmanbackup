Refund policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

Who this covers

This policy applies to digital kits sold by Best Man Backup through our Etsy shop. Purchases are paid for and delivered on Etsy.

Contact: [contact email — SEAN TO FILL], or message the BestManBackup shop on Etsy.

What you are buying

Our products are instant digital downloads (Word and PDF files), not physical goods. After checkout, Etsy makes the files available to you immediately.

Your right to cancel

If you are a consumer in the UK or the EU, you normally have 14 days to cancel an online purchase. For digital content, that right ends once both of the following are true:

  1. you have given your consent for us to start delivery before the 14 days are up; and
  2. you have acknowledged that you will lose the right to cancel once the download begins.

Etsy’s checkout is designed to collect that consent for instant digital downloads. If you have already downloaded the files, we will not normally refund the order just because you have changed your mind or no longer need the kit.

When we will help or refund

We will put things right, or refund you, if:

  • the download fails or a file is corrupted, and we cannot give you a working copy;
  • the files you received are not the product described on the listing (for example, a missing cue-card set on the Speech Kit);
  • we have made a genuine error on our side.

To help us, message the shop with your Etsy order number and a short description of the problem. We aim to reply within two working days.

When we will not normally refund

  • You have successfully downloaded the files and then changed your mind.
  • You bought the wrong kit.
  • You cannot open a Word or PDF file because of your own software or device, after we have told you what format the files use.
  • You wanted a speech written for you. Our kits are templates and guides you personalise yourself; they are not a ghost-writing service.

How a refund is paid

If we agree a refund, it is processed through Etsy, back to the original payment method, in line with Etsy’s timing.

Chargebacks

If something is wrong, please contact us first so we can fix it. A chargeback is still your right where the card issuer allows it, but it is slower for everyone than a message to the shop.
