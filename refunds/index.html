The 14-day right

Under the Consumer Contracts (Information, Cancellation and Additional Charges) Regulations 2013, a consumer who buys at a distance normally has 14 days to cancel. The 14 days start the day after the contract is made.

Digital content

These kits are digital content. They are not supplied on a tangible medium, and nothing is posted.

Regulation 37 says that cancellation right is lost for this kind of digital content only if, before the supply begins, the consumer has done both of these:

  • given express consent for the supply to begin during the cancellation period, and
  • acknowledged that they lose the right to cancel once the supply has begun.

The trader must also confirm that consent and that acknowledgement on a durable medium, with the confirmation of the contract.
